# Промпты карусели «Садись и выдохни» — 18–29, «Чилл»

Под GPT Image 2. Направление — **Mobility OS Editorial, спокойный вечерний вариант** ([[ВИЗУАЛЬНАЯ-СИСТЕМА-2026]]).

Kinetic Moscow для этого сегмента не берём: оранжево-красная энергия и ощущение скорости противоречат анти-скоростному посылу тарифа «Чилл». Daylight — линия объяснения, она пойдёт на стадию «понимание».

## Как запускать

1. **Общий блок копируется целиком в каждый из пяти запросов, без правок.** Это единственный рычаг, который держит серию одной серией: параметра `seed` у моделей изображений нет, воспроизводимость недостижима
2. Первым генерится кадр 1. Он якорь: пока он не утверждён, остальные четыре не запускать
3. Утверждённый кадр 1 прикладывается **референсом** к запросам 2–5. Не «в стиле как раньше», а файлом
4. Правки — цепочкой от утверждённого кадра, не генерацией с нуля. Генерация заново уводит стиль
5. Формат 4:5. Финальные файлы 1080×1350

## Общий блок — копировать целиком

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: finished vertical 4:5 Russian campaign key visual for a
contemporary Moscow robotaxi brand. Part of a five-card carousel series.

Direction: Mobility OS Editorial — calm evening variant.

Build a complete art-directed composition, not a photograph with text on top.
Use 5-7 overlapping modules of different depth: headline, vehicle, Moscow
context, motion, structural geometry, one accent. Integrate photography and
typography with clean masks and controlled overlap. Keep corner radii,
shadows and spacing identical across the whole series.

Palette: midnight navy base, cobalt blue, clean warm white, one yellow
electric car, soft teal-green light as the single accent. No lime, no orange,
no red, no purple — this is the calm line of the campaign.

Mood: unhurried, quiet, blue hour. Motion may appear only as calm flowing
light, never as speed, racing or urgency.

Typography: large modern Cyrillic grotesque, one typographic trick per layout,
strict optical alignment. Spell every letter exactly as specified. No extra
words, no invented slogans, no watermarks.

Vehicle: the same unbranded yellow electric crossover in every card. No logos,
no badges, no licence plates, no visible steering wheel.

Moscow: Moscow-City towers, modern embankments, wet asphalt with reflections,
large architectural fragments. Real geography only.

STRICTLY EXCLUDE: metro symbols, metro maps, coordinates, road numbers,
navigation pins, fake interface, fake dashboards, invented figures or data,
real logos or any resemblance to Yandex identity, torn paper, vintage or
grunge texture, glassmorphism, purple neon, sci-fi HUD, an empty driver's
seat or any framing that reads as "nobody is driving", faces in close-up,
QR codes.
```

## Кадр 1 — якорь серии

**Текст в кадре:** заголовок `САДИСЬ И ВЫДОХНИ`, плашка внизу `МОСКВА`

```text
Composition: the yellow electric crossover in three-quarter rear view, moving
calmly along a Moscow embankment at blue hour. Moscow-City towers stand behind
it, slightly out of focus, their lights reflected in wet asphalt.

The headline "САДИСЬ И ВЫДОХНИ" is set in three stacked lines in the upper
left, large and confident, warm white. The single typographic trick: the word
"ВЫДОХНИ" is filled with soft teal-green and sits on its own darker module,
so it reads as the exhale of the whole frame. A thin teal line runs from the
end of that word and continues into the light trail behind the car, tying
typography and photography into one object.

A small navy chip in the lower left carries the word "МОСКВА" in small spaced
capitals. Generous negative space on the right side. The car may overlap the
lower edge of the headline module, but every letter stays readable.
```

## Кадр 2 — утро

**Текст в кадре:** заголовок `УТРО БЕЗ ШУМА`, подпись внизу `УТРЕННИЙ МАРШРУТ`

```text
Composition: early morning, cool pre-sunrise light over a quiet Moscow street.
The same yellow crossover waits at the kerb, seen from a low front-quarter
angle, one door open toward the viewer. No person in frame.

The headline "УТРО БЕЗ ШУМА" runs across the middle in two lines, warm white.
The trick here is contour: the word "ШУМА" is outlined only, hollow, so the
city shows through it — the noise is literally empty. Behind the headline, two
soft navy modules hold the composition together.

Footer strip in small spaced capitals: "УТРЕННИЙ МАРШРУТ". Keep the frame
almost silent: no traffic, no crowd, no motion blur.
```

## Кадр 3 — тихая капсула

**Текст в кадре:** заголовок `ТИХАЯ КАПСУЛА`, подпись внизу `СВОЙ ПЛЕЙЛИСТ. СВОЙ СВЕТ.`

```text
Composition: interior of the car, shot from the rear seat looking sideways and
slightly down. Only the rear cabin is visible: seat texture, a phone resting
face down, a bag on the seat, the side window with blurred evening Moscow
sliding past. Soft teal-green LED light runs along the cabin perimeter and
picks out the edges of the seats. Warm reflections on the glass.

Crucially: the front row, the steering wheel and the driver's seat are outside
the frame entirely. This is a rear-cabin crop, not a view of an empty cockpit.

The headline "ТИХАЯ КАПСУЛА" sits in the upper third on a navy module, warm
white, with the word "ТИХАЯ" slightly lighter in weight than "КАПСУЛА".
Footer in small spaced capitals: "СВОЙ ПЛЕЙЛИСТ. СВОЙ СВЕТ."

This card is the quietest of the five: fewer modules, more photograph, one
accent of light.
```

## Кадр 4 — короткая поездка

**Текст в кадре:** заголовок `15 МИНУТ ДЛЯ СЕБЯ`, подпись внизу `ПОСРЕДИ ДНЯ`

```text
Composition: daytime, overcast cool light. The yellow crossover is seen in a
tight side crop against a large modern architectural fragment — a facade of
glass and concrete, no signage.

The number "15" is the main typographic object: very large, warm white, partly
overlapped by the car, occupying the left half of the frame. The rest of the
headline, "МИНУТ ДЛЯ СЕБЯ", is set small and precise, aligned to the baseline
of the number. A teal underline sits beneath the words "ДЛЯ СЕБЯ".

Footer in small spaced capitals: "ПОСРЕДИ ДНЯ".

The card must read as time, not as a discount: no price signs, no currency,
no percentage marks, no burst or sale graphics anywhere in the frame.
```

## Кадр 5 — подпись серии

**Текст в кадре:** `ГОРОД СНАРУЖИ. ТИШИНА ВНУТРИ.`

```text
Composition: a two-part frame divided by a clean vertical mask. The left part
is the city: Moscow-City at blue hour, light trails, wet asphalt, dense and
alive. The right part is the calm interior: soft teal-green light, the curve
of a seat, quiet shadow. The yellow car crosses the division at the bottom of
the frame and stitches both halves into one composition.

The line "ГОРОД СНАРУЖИ. ТИШИНА ВНУТРИ." is set in two lines across the lower
third, warm white. The trick: the word "СНАРУЖИ" sits on the city side and the
word "ТИШИНА" on the interior side, so the typography carries the same split
as the image. Keep the type large enough to read in one second.

No footer chip on this card. This is the closing frame of the series.
```

## Чек-лист приёмки — по каждому кадру

Не прошло хотя бы одно — кадр не считается готовым и перегенерируется целевой итерацией, без смены направления.

- [ ] Кириллица побуквенно совпадает с `ТЕКСТЫ.md`. Проверять глазами, а не по памяти
- [ ] Главный тезис читается за секунду
- [ ] 5–7 осмысленных слоёв, не визуальный шум и не набор одинаковых плиток
- [ ] Автомобиль один и тот же во всех пяти кадрах
- [ ] Нет руля, переднего ряда и пустого водительского места
- [ ] Нет метро, координат, карт, псевдоинтерфейса и выдуманных показателей
- [ ] Нет логотипов и сходства с айдентикой Яндекса
- [ ] Нет оранжевого, красного, лайма и фиолетового: линия спокойная
- [ ] Ничего не читается как скорость или срочность
- [ ] На кадре 4 нет знаков цены, процентов и скидочной графики
- [ ] Кадр выглядит частью серии, а не отдельной картинкой

```bash
cd "РЕЗУЛЬТАТЫ/06_контент/2026-07-31_карусель-чилл-18-29"
python3 - <<'PY'
from PIL import Image
import pathlib
src = sorted(pathlib.Path("final_4x5").glob("*.png"))
w, h, gap = 432, 540, 16
sheet = Image.new("RGB", (w*len(src) + gap*(len(src)-1), h), "#0B1220")
for i, p in enumerate(src):
    sheet.paste(Image.open(p).resize((w, h)), (i*(w+gap), 0))
sheet.save("превью-сет-5-карточек.jpg", quality=92)
print("готово:", len(src), "кадров")
PY
```
