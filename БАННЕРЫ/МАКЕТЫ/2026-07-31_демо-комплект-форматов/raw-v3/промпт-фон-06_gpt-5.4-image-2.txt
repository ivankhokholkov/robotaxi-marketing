Advertising background plate for a Moscow robotaxi campaign. Typography will be
composited separately in code, so the image itself must contain NO TEXT AT ALL.

Native tall vertical composition. Blue hour just after rain in a modern Moscow
residential district (ZILART), wet asphalt with long light reflections.

Structure by height:

UPPER PART, roughly the top half — a matte near-black editorial field, flat and
uniform, with a faint matte paper texture and nothing else in it: no
architecture, no objects, no detail. This is reserved negative space for
typography. One saturated red kinetic plane cuts diagonally through it from the
top right corner as the only structural gesture.

The boundary between the dark field and the photograph below is ONE CLEAN
DIAGONAL EDGE running down to the left. Never a horizontal line.

LOWER PART, roughly the bottom half — the photographic act: a rear
three-quarter view of a white autonomous taxi driving away to the right, large
in frame. It is a Hyundai Sonata style FASTBACK SEDAN: long bonnet, low
fast-sloping rear roofline, a proper boot deck, a slim full-width red tail-light
strip, large multi-spoke alloy wheels, low sedan stance. On the roof a low white
sensor shell with a black band and a short black lidar drum on top. The livery
is a Yandex style pattern of angular arrow shapes: sharp red arrows high on the
rear quarter panel, yellow arrows in the middle of the doors, lime and green
arrows lower. White body everywhere else. Not a Polestar, not a Tesla, not a
crossover, not a hatchback.

Behind it, real modern residential blocks with warm window lights against a cool
blue-grey evening sky. A restrained turquoise glow along the edge of the
windscreen glass only — a thin line of light, not paint, not a light bar. The
body reads as cool grey-white in evening light, never blown-out white. A few
small livery-coloured polygons sit along the very bottom edge as controlled
markers.

Photographic, editorial quality, 35–50mm equivalent, natural perspective, soft
directional light, cool-neutral white balance with a faint teal cast.

STRICTLY EXCLUDE: any text, letters, words, numerals, captions, signage,
watermarks or logos of any kind; any user interface, app screen, map overlay,
navigation pin, HUD, gauge, crosshair or pseudo-data; QR codes; metro symbols;
recognisable faces; an empty driver's seat or any framing that reads as
"nobody is driving"; purple or violet neon; glassmorphism; torn paper; grunge;
vintage cars; roof cargo boxes; glowing roof bars; centred symmetrical hero
posters; oversaturated plastic CGI.
