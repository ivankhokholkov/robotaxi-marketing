# Промпты по семействам форматов

> **Что это:** заготовки промптов под каждое семейство пропорций. Стиль и полный шаблон запроса — [[СТИЛЬ-Kinetic-Moscow]] и [[ЧТО-ОТПРАВЛЯТЬ-В-ГЕНЕРАЦИЮ]]; здесь только то, что меняется от формата к формату. Сверено 01.08.2026.
>
> **Про `Exact headline` в блоках 4–8.** Форматы РСЯ, UrbanAds и Карт идут путём «накладка кодом»: модель делает фон и композиционный черновик, текст кладётся слоем. Для них строка `Exact headline` из промпта **убирается** и заменяется на `no text, no letters, no words anywhere in the frame`, а место под заголовок задаётся композиционно. Оставлять её можно только в блоках 1–3 (SMM). Какой формат каким путём идёт — `formats.yaml`, поле `путь_текста`.

## Общий стилевой блок

```text
Use case: production advertising concept for Moscow robotaxi.
Build an art-directed product composition, not a photo with a caption.

Visual language: mature mobility-tech editorial design. Matte near-black or
midnight navy base with a barely visible precision grid, warm white modern
Cyrillic grotesque, the real white fastback robotaxi from the supplied
references, compact wide roof sensor pod and short lidar tower, authentic
red-yellow-green livery fragments, one controlled red accent and one thin
cool-blue sensing or motion line.

Technology comes from exact grid, realistic product photography, clean masks,
optical alignment, controlled overlap and physical light. No fake interface,
no HUD, no invented data, no metro symbols, no navigation map, no torn paper,
no vintage texture, no purple neon, no glassmorphism, no random icons.

The real vehicle references are authoritative. Keep the same body, headlights,
grille, wheels, roof sensor module and livery placement. Moscow location must
match the supplied location reference. No invented landmark geometry.
```

## 1. SMM 4:5 — 1080×1350

```text
Finished Russian campaign key visual, vertical 4:5. Use 6–8 meaningful depth
layers. Place the exact headline “МОСКВА ВИДИТ БОЛЬШЕ” in two or three stacked
lines in the upper left. The white robotaxi crosses the headline module and a
large Moscow photographic window. Add one sensor macro detail, one fine grid,
one cool-blue trajectory line and one restrained red underline. Preserve clear
negative space and exact Cyrillic. The layout must still read at phone size.
```

## 2. SMM square — 1080×1080 / UrbanAds pop-up — 720×720

```text
Square product campaign composition. Use 5–6 layers. Headline “РОБОТАКСИ В
МОСКВЕ” occupies the upper-left quadrant. The car is a three-quarter front
hero object crossing the center. One cropped Moscow architecture module sits
behind it. The sensor pod receives a precise cool highlight. Keep all critical
elements in the central safe area and do not draw a fake call-to-action button.
```

## 3. Story / vertical UrbanAds — 1080×1920 or 940×1524

```text
Tall vertical mobility campaign. Use 6–8 layers with three scales: Moscow
establishing view, full vehicle in motion, sensor macro. Headline “МОСКВА ВИДИТ
БОЛЬШЕ” is large and stacked above the car. Use the lower section for the
vehicle and a controlled motion line, but keep the bottom 232 pixels free of
critical information for the 940×1524 UrbanAds version. No small copy.
```

## 4. UrbanAds horizontal — 1080×450

```text
Wide 12:5 product banner. Use 4–5 layers. Exact headline “РОБОТАКСИ В МОСКВЕ”
on the left in no more than two lines. Full vehicle three-quarter view on the
right, overlapping one dark architecture module. Add only a fine grid and one
red-to-yellow livery accent. Keep every meaningful element inside a centered
990×410 safe area. No button and no small print.
```

## 5. Maps — 1280×256 / UrbanAds stretch — 1706×184 / low RSYA strips

```text
Ultra-wide low-height mobility banner. Use only 3–4 layers. Exact short
headline “РОБОТАКСИ В МОСКВЕ” on the left-center. On the right use a clean
side crop of the real robotaxi or a macro view of the roof sensor pod. Add a
subtle precision grid and one thin cool-blue sensing line ending in a small red
point. Dark background only. Minimum-detail composition readable in one
second. No secondary paragraph, no icons, no borders, no fake interface.
```

For the 1280×256 Maps version keep at least **60 px empty at BOTH the left and
the right edge** for the automatic “Реклама” label and service elements. For
1706×184 keep everything inside 1564×184.

## 6. РСЯ 300×600 / 160×600

```text
Narrow vertical performance banner derived from the same campaign. Use 3–5
layers. Stack “МОСКВА / ВИДИТ / БОЛЬШЕ” at the top. Show the real robotaxi in a
dynamic side or three-quarter crop through the middle. Use one vertical cool
line and a single red accent. Remove all secondary facts. Maintain strong
silhouette and readable Cyrillic at actual banner size.
```

## 7. РСЯ 300×250 / 300×300

```text
Compact product banner with one-message hierarchy. Exact headline “РОБОТАКСИ
В МОСКВЕ”. The white car occupies the lower-right half. Use one Moscow fragment
as a dark masked background and one restrained red underline. Maximum 4–5
layers, no decorative data and no embedded button.
```

## 8. РСЯ 970×250

```text
Wide desktop banner. Headline “МОСКВА ВИДИТ БОЛЬШЕ” on the left, the real
robotaxi in side motion across the middle, one recognizable Moscow fragment on
the right. Use 3–4 layers, subtle grid and one cool motion line. Keep text to
one line when possible and avoid tiny explanatory copy.
```

## Производственный режим

Для SMM можно запросить у модели точный текст внутри изображения и затем проверить каждую букву. Для UrbanAds, Карт и РСЯ этот промпт используется как art-direction draft: финальный текст и охранные зоны собираются в точном холсте по SVG-гайду из `СЕТКИ/`.
